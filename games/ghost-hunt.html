👻 Ghost Hunt Instructions

🎯 Objective

You are a brave pumpkin exploring a haunted night. Collect candy for points while dodging hungry ghosts!

🎮 How to Play

  1. Start: Press SPACE or click Start Game
  2. Move: Use WASD (or arrow keys) to roam the dark
  3. Collect: Grab candy 🍬 for points
  4. Survive: Don't let ghosts touch you
  5. Waves: Each wave adds more ghosts and faster chases

⌨️ Controls

  • WASD / Arrows: Move
  • SPACE: Start / Pause

🏆 Scoring

  • Candy: +10 points each
  • Wave Bonus: +50 when you clear a wave
  • High Score: Saved on your device

💡 Tips

  • Keep moving — standing still makes you easy prey
  • Use the edges to kite ghosts around
  • Grab candy quickly before the next ghost spawns
